# Release verification

Verified on 2026-10-09 with Node.js 24 and headless Chrome through Playwright.

- 59 tests passed: 32 engine/source-asset checks, 14 articulated-motion checks, 3 generated-motion asset checks, 6 impact-effect checks and 4 rendered-court projection/asset checks. Engine checks include all ten identities, flight and landing, manual perfect spikes, hit stop, dedicated blocking, faults, manual movement even with a legacy assist flag, stopping after input ends, every difficulty and character, automatic tosses/attack jumps without changing player position, buffered manual spikes, and correct attack ownership. Active match fixtures supply direction input explicitly.
- The production bundle was built and exercised at `http://localhost:5173/dist/`, including its repository-style subpath.
- Real browser checks passed for 10 selectable original characters, partner changes, stored selection and settings after reload, manual movement that stops after input is released, keyboard block/charge, joystick capture, pause/resume, help, automatic jumping after manual positioning followed by an actual manual spike, the actual 60-second practice result, dark mode, and reduced motion. The former movement assistance setting is absent; a saved enabled preference is removed without losing victory records.
- An 800ms touch hold charged and released the attack without selecting text or opening a popup. An ordinary sound button also passed a real 800ms touch hold. Delegated context-menu, selection and drag guards were checked on buttons, labels, images, links and the canvas. Real inputs, textareas, selects and contenteditable descendants retained native editing. Native iOS callouts are disabled in CSS; these automated browser checks ran in Chrome.
- In the descending-toss timing fixture every character had 0.625–0.658 seconds of valid attack time. The manual input buffer lasts 0.65 seconds. Tosses use a slower arc, and attack jumps include a brief hang near the apex.
- Screen sizes: 320×568, 320×720, 360×780, 393×852, 430×932, 844×390, and desktop 1440×1000. No horizontal overflow; the mobile start button remains visible. Portrait pages have at most 1px of rounding overflow.
- The court redraws immediately after a canvas resize. Captures wait for painted canvas pixels and use the actual viewport, including portrait-to-landscape changes.
- No page exceptions, console errors, or failed HTTP responses were observed in the final browser checks.
- The six Blender stadium/net plates total 515,586 bytes. Day, sunset and floodlit night were displayed in the actual mobile app. Eight independently measured Blender camera points match the 2D game projection within 0.000106 pixels at the original render size; WebP dimensions and transparent net layers are verified.
- Impact effects have distinct toss/set/spike/block silhouettes, follow the outgoing ball and reproject on resize. Tests verify expiry, particle/trail limits and reduced motion. The actual manually triggered spike is captured with its contact spark; the ball stays visible above the effect.
- All four local recorded-sound files decoded in the normal mobile game and after an offline reload. Samples total 440,767 bytes; source pages, authors, CC0 terms and hashes are retained in `assets/audio/SOURCES.md`.
- The optional `audio-browser-qa.mjs` analyser probe did not complete. No measured final-bus amplitude, bus-isolation or subjective listening result is claimed.
- Offline reload and gameplay were verified with a first-visit cache, including selection of a character outside the initial team and all four sound decodes. Another app's cache was preserved.
- A same-browser staged v5→v6 test exercised an actual stale v5 HTML cache response. The explicit v6 link reloaded exactly once into the new directional-animation game. First installation did not reload; an existing v5 tab was not navigated; old game caches were removed and another app's cache was preserved. Online navigation fetches current HTML, with offline cache fallback.
- New generated directional kits total 5,044,914 bytes. All ten authoring PNGs have real alpha transparency; 440 production parts have non-empty pixels, valid local joints and overlapping limb cuts. WebP alpha is preserved exactly; source/alpha/WebP hashes are retained in the motion manifest. Original PNGs are excluded from the production bundle. The app keeps only its current four decoded motion atlases.
- Built-in image_gen generated new front, rear, left and right artwork for all ten original identities. Raven’s original black leggings and Viper’s original bare hands were repaired with additional generation. Original portraits remain unchanged. The prompt set and saved authoring files are in `assets/motions/PROMPTS.md`.
- Seven authored motion clips each contain 60 unique joint poses with fixed limb lengths, continuous interpolation and 120ms action transitions. Real browser rendering checked 2,400 sequential movement frames (10 identities × 4 directions × 60), drawing all 11 parts per actor. Every direction produced 60 distinct raster frames; maximum per-sample joint step was below 14 world units. Another 1,800 toss/spike/block samples checked 60 unique joint poses and at least 57 distinct rendered frames per clip, including held preparations until contact. Actual key movement captured 60 consecutive frames in each direction at a mean interval of 16.7ms on the tested desktop Chrome instance. This is a local rendering measurement, not a guarantee for every mobile device.
- Idle/run wrists, a forward relaxed-elbow pole and all-direction billboard depth were corrected after visual inspection so elbows stay below shoulders and no forearm crosses above the crown. A serve preparation continues into the same contact clip without a delayed idle transition. Hair crown alignment, SD body proportions, reachable contact IK and refusal to stretch arms toward distant contacts have regression checks. Contact ranges remain generous arcade rules; exact hand/ball alignment is not claimed for contacts beyond anatomical reach.
- Both the physical Blender assembly and the engine tape are exactly twice their original height: 2.43m→4.86m and z230→z460. Blocking requires close net distance, a narrow lateral lane and matching hand height; wrong positions and premature/low contacts fail, including the block specialist. Both teams’ serves and all ten airborne attacks can clear the doubled tape on ordinary ballistic arcs.
- Original three-frame character sheets total 1,318,542 bytes after proportional mobile optimization. The ten original-face thumbnails total 107,758 bytes. The original identities, designs, poses, transparency, and source provenance are retained.
- The initial release's Lighthouse mobile simulation measured performance 86, accessibility 100, best practices 100, SEO 100, total blocking time 0ms and layout shift 0. Its conservative slow-network LCP estimate was 4.2s. This benchmark was not repeated for this presentation update.
